Lesson 1 · 18 min

The map of generators in Oct 2026 and how to choose

Video generators change versions every few months and sometimes disappear, like Sora. That's why the choice doesn't start with the ranking: it starts with what your shot asks for. Duration, dialogue, reference, and license decide; the dated table just shows who meets the need today.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The five questions that choose the generator for a shot
  2. 2What each major generator offers in Oct 2026, with source and date
  3. 3Why the Sora case teaches you to keep everything outside the platform
  4. 4Record the choice in a file that the page checks

Goal: choose the generator for each shot based on what it asks for (duration, dialogue, reference, license) and record the choice with the date.

01 · Five questions before choosing

The shot decides the generator, not the other way around. Click each question.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

Answer all five for the shot, not for the whole film: different shots can go to different generators.

02 · The generators as of Oct 2026 (table updated on 10/19/2026)

Only what has a dated source. Anything not confirmed appears as "reported." Tap each one.

1. Veo 3.1

Native audio, with dialogue and effects. Accepts up to 3 reference images (the "Ingredients" feature), first-to-last frame transition, and scene extension.

Duration per clip: the numbers going around come from third parties and were not confirmed on an official page. Veo 4 has not been announced.

SourceGoogle developers blog, Veo 3.1 launch (Oct 2025).

2. Kling 3.0 and 3.0 Omni

Released in February 2026: up to 15 s per generation, native audio in several languages, and dialogue with more than one character.

Omni controls duration, angle, and movement shot by shot. The character "elements" and the cut limit per generation are reported by third parties, without official confirmation.

SourceKling AI announcement (02/05/2026); Adobe blog on Firefly (04/15/2026).

3. Seedance 2.5

Up to 30 s with audio generated in the same pass. Accepts up to 30 images, 10 videos, and 10 audios as references.

It replaced 2.0. Version 2.0 caused controversy with the US film studios' association for generating real actors and characters: a reminder that references have an ethical limit (course 2).

SourceThe Decoder (08/01/2026).

4. Runway Gen-4.5 and Aleph 2.0

Gen-4.5 gained native audio, multiple shots in one generation, and videos up to 1 minute in December 2025.

Aleph 2.0 edits a video that already exists by text: remove or add an object, swap the background, change the light. The release date of Aleph 2.0 is reported, not confirmed.

SourceTechCrunch (12/11/2025).

5. Luma Ray3.14

Ray3.14 (January 2026) generates in native 1080p, faster and cheaper than the previous one.

Ray3 Modify changes footage based on a character image and start and end frames. Native audio in Ray: not confirmed.

SourceBusiness Wire (01/26/2026); TechCrunch (12/18/2025).

6. Wan 2.2

The last Wan with open weights, under Apache 2.0. Versions 2.6, 2.7, and 3.0 exist only as a closed service.

The 5B version runs on 8 GB VRAM cards with ComfyUI's memory offloading, according to the official documentation. Details in lesson 7.

SourceComfyUI documentation (checked on 10/19/2026).

7. LTX-2.3 and LTX-2.5

Open weights with video and audio. LTX-2.5 brings several shots in one generation. The LTX-2.x community license allows commercial use at no cost below US$ 10 million in revenue per year.

The ComfyUI documentation calls for a card with 32 GB or more of VRAM and 100 GB of free disk space.

SourceLTX-2.5 page on Hugging Face and LTX documentation (checked on 10/19/2026).

8. Sora 2: a case study

The app closed on 04/26/2026 and the API on 09/24/2026. Anyone who kept their script, references and prompts only inside it lost the way back.

The lesson applies to any generator: everything that is yours stays in a folder outside the platform, as text. No lesson tells you to use Sora.

SourceOpenAI help center on the Sora shutdown (2026).

Only for people who already use a terminal. Download the course from GitHub and run it in its folder: the rules are the same as on the page. For your file, replace --exemplo with its name (escolha.txt); it exits 0 when it passes.

06 · Practice

Use the shot list or storyboard you made in course 0. If you didn't make one, use shot 4 from "The first batch".

Choice · one shot from your scene

20 min

Take the hardest shot in your shot list (the one with dialogue, or the one that needs the same character). Answer the five questions and fill in the escolha.txt using the template. Open the page of the tool you chose and note the date you checked it. Paste it into "Check your choice" and use "Download the file" to save it in the project folder.

Done when: the page answers OK: choice with 8 fields and the escolha.txt is in the project folder, next to geradores-out-2026.txt.

Portability · the folder outside the platform

10 min

Check that the project folder has, in text, everything you would need to redo the scene in another generator: storyboard, character sheet, look, and the escolha.txt. Anything that only exists inside a platform, copy it to the folder now.

Done when: the project folder has storyboard.txt, ficha.txt, look.txt and escolha.txt, and all four open in a plain text editor, with no account on any platform.
