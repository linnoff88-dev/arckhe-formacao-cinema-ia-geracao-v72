Your prompt generator: from storyboard to prompt list with an agent

Writing eight prompts by hand, with the same look and the same lock, is tiring and leaves room for mistakes. An AI assistant with clear rules drafts the whole list from the storyboard in course 0. You review it, the page checks it, and the list goes to any generator.

02 · Building your prompt generator

The example turns the 30-second storyboard of "The first batch" into a list.

1. Why an assistant

Eight shots call for eight prompts with the same base prompt and the same lock, word for word. By hand, one forgotten comma changes the look of a shot.

A request with fixed rules makes the assistant repeat what has to stay the same and change only what changes. You swap the storyboard and reuse the request.

2. The six layers of the request

Role (who it is), input (the pasted files), output format (the exact line), vocabulary (the camera terms from course 0), rules (copy the base prompt and lock, sound in a separate sentence) and limits (what to do when information is missing).

The limits layer is the one that protects you most: instead of making things up, the assistant writes "FALTA:" and what was missing.

3. Review the output

The page checks format and numbers. You read looking for what wasn't in the storyboard: an extra character, a new object, a swapped line of dialogue.

Found something made up? Don't just fix the line: add the missing rule to the request, so it doesn't come back with the next storyboard.

4. From file to generator

To generate, paste the base prompt, the lock, and the shot line, and attach the references for that shot. If the generator wants a different way of writing, adjust the line, not the list.

The list, the storyboard, the lookbook, and the references stay in the folder. If the generator changes or shuts down, you take everything to another one.

03 · The layered request and the list format

Paste the request into your AI assistant's chat from your subscription, with the three files at the end.

Request to the AI assistant (prompt writer)
ROLE: You are a directing assistant and you write video prompts for generators with audio.
INPUT: a storyboard (one line per shot), a lookbook, and a list of references, pasted at the end.
OUTPUT: first the lines "Scene:", "Base prompt:" and "Lock:"; then one line per shot, in this format:
n | duration | shot size | movement | prompt: <camera, subject, action, setting> | sound: <ambience, effect, dialogue as someone saying: text>
VOCABULARY: size, angle, and movement only with the terms from the storyboard (wide shot, close-up, high camera, dolly in, static camera...).
RULES:
- copy the Base prompt from the lookbook and the Lock from the references word for word;
- one movement per shot, the same as in the storyboard;
- sound in its own sentence, after "sound:";
- do not mention a director, film, brand, or real person.
LIMITS: do not invent a character, place, object, or line of dialogue that is not in the input. If something is missing, write "MISSING:" and what was missing.
Storyboard:
<paste the storyboard.txt here>
Lookbook:
<paste the lookbook.txt here>
References:
<paste the referencias.txt here>
prompts.txt (template)
Scene: <name> (<duration> s)
Base prompt: <from lookbook.txt, word for word>
Lock: <from referencias.txt, word for word>
<n> | <n> s | <shot size> | <one movement> | prompt: <camera, subject, action, setting> | sound: <ambience, effect, dialogue>
