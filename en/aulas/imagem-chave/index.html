Lesson 3 · 18 min

Key image first: cinematic still and lookbook

Before you animate, get the picture right. An image takes seconds, so you can test the look many times and take only the approved frame into video. The lookbook keeps the recipe for that look in a base prompt that goes into every still of the scene unchanged.

Start the lesson

02 · Lookbook and image generators

The example follows "The first batch".

1. Why the image comes first

Getting the look wrong in a video costs a whole generation. In an image, you change the light, the lens, and the palette in minutes and compare them side by side.

The approved frame is the anchor of the scene: it carries the character, the place, the palette, and the light. Everything that comes after inherits from it.

2. The ingredients of the look

Palette (two or three dominant colors), lens (wide-angle, normal, or tele), light (from the look card in course 0), texture (film grain, contrast), and aspect ratio of the frame.

Describe the ingredient, don't name a director or a film: "in the style of so-and-so" imitates a real person's work and changes depending on how the generator understands the name.

Batch looknight blue on the street, tile white, orange only in the fire; 35 mm; low-key with side light from the oven; fine grain; 16:9

3. Base prompt

Put the ingredients together in one line that goes into every still the same way. Leave a marked space for what changes from shot to shot: the subject and the action.

If the generator accepts a style reference image, use the first approved still as the reference for the others (lesson 5 goes deeper).

4. Image generators (Oct/2026)

Open: FLUX.2 [klein] (Jan/2026). The 4B is Apache 2.0 and runs on about 8 GB of VRAM; the 9B has a non-commercial license. It generates, edits, and accepts multiple reference images, according to the official repository.

Closed: Midjourney V8.1 came out on 04/30/2026, according to its documentation. GPT Image 2 and Nano Banana Pro are cited by third-party sites as strong in 2026, with no official confirmation checked here.

SourceOfficial FLUX.2 repository on GitHub (checked on 10/19/2026).

03 · Templates to copy

The lookbook that the page checks and the request for the AI assistant to build the base prompt.

lookbook.txt (template)
Palette: <2 or 3 dominant colors and where they appear>
Lens: <wide-angle, 35 mm, 50 mm, tele; shallow or deep focus>
Light: <your look card from course 0>
Texture: <grain, contrast, sharpness>
Aspect ratio: <16:9, 9:16, 2.39:1...>
Base prompt: <everything above in one line, with [SUBJECT AND ACTION] in place of what changes>
Key shots: <numbers of the shots that become stills>
Request to the AI assistant (base prompt)
You are a cinematographer. With the look card and the character sheet below, write a cinematic image base prompt, in one line, to repeat in every still of the scene.
Rules:
- describe palette, lens, light, texture, and aspect ratio with concrete words;
- do not name a director, film, photographer, or brand;
- leave the [SUBJECT AND ACTION] placeholder where what changes from shot to shot goes.
Look card:
<paste the look.txt here>
Character sheet:
<paste the ficha.txt here>

06 · Practice

Use the look.txt and the ficha.txt from course 0, or the first-batch recipe, as a starting point.

Lookbook · the recipe for your scene

20 min

Fill in the lookbook.txt using the template, with no director, film, or brand names. Pick three key shots from your shot list. Paste it into "Check your lookbook" and download the file to the project folder.

Done when: the page answers OK: lookbook with 7 fields and the lookbook.txt is in the project folder.

Stills · three key shots

30 min

In whatever image generator you have (open or closed), generate the three key shots with the same base prompt, changing only the [SUBJECT AND ACTION]. Save the approved one for each with the shot number: plano-01, plano-04, plano-08.

Done when: the project folder has the three image files with the shot number in the name, and the prompt for each one starts with the same Base prompt from the lookbook.txt.
