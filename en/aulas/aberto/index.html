Lesson 7 · 18 min

The open path: ComfyUI with LTX and Wan 2.2, VRAM and license

An open model runs on your machine, with no credit per generation and no need for a platform to stay online. In return, it asks for a graphics card with memory, free disk space and a read of the license. This lesson helps you decide whether your machine can run it and which model and template to use in ComfyUI.

Start the lesson

02 · ComfyUI, models and licenses

Numbers from the official documentation, checked on 10/19/2026.

1. What ComfyUI is

A free program, with a window, that builds the generation as boxes connected by lines (nodes): load model, write prompt, load image, generate, save. Each box does one thing.

You don't have to build it from scratch: the official templates come ready, and the download-models button fetches the files that are missing.

2. How much your machine can handle

Wan 2.2 5B (text and image to video): fits in 8 GB of VRAM with ComfyUI's memory offloading, according to the ComfyUI documentation. The 14B has image-to-video and first and last frame templates.

LTX-2.5: the documentation asks for a CUDA-compatible card, 32 GB or more of VRAM and 100 GB free; there are smaller files (INT8) for cards with less memory.

Sourcesdocs.comfy.org (Wan 2.2) and docs.ltx.io (ComfyUI with LTX), checked on 10/19/2026.

3. Read the license

Apache 2.0 (Wan 2.2, FLUX.2 [klein] 4B): commercial use allowed. LTX-2.x community license: commercial use free below US$ 10 million in revenue per year; above that, a paid license; passing on fine-tunes may require a paid license.

Non-commercial (FLUX.2 [klein] 9B): research and personal use. ARCKHÉ content is free, but your work may not be: check before you start.

4. Templates and the LTX notice

The LTX-2.5 templates come with a "prompt enhancer" (Prompt Enhance) turned on by default, which rewrites your text before generating. To use exactly the prompt from lesson 2, turn this option off.

The LTX-2.5 first and last frame template skips the upscaling step; the text and image-to-video ones use two steps.

Sourcedocs.ltx.io, "Using ComfyUI with LTX" (checked on 10/19/2026).
